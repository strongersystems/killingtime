The deal

What we want from you

  • Be there for the nights you signed up for, invited by the time we pull.
  • Come repaired, flasked and having watched the fight.
  • Take it well when we show you your own log.
  • Be in Discord. You do not have to talk.

How we run it

  • Two teams. If the CE nights do not suit, the six-hour team raids fewer of them.
  • Every pull we have ever logged is on the tracker, and so is every one of yours.
  • Bench decisions get explained, with the logs open.
  • Nobody is made to do alt runs.
