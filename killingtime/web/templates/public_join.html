What we ask, what you get

What we ask

  • Turn up. {% if schedule.days %}{{ schedule.nights_per_week }} nights a week{% else %}Raid nights{% endif %}, on time, repaired and consumed.
  • Know the fight before the pull, and know your own cooldowns better than we do.
  • Take feedback like an adult. We read the logs, and we will show you yours.
  • Be someone the raid wants on Discord at 23:00 on a wipe night.

What you get

  • A roster that shows up, and two teams so there is a night that fits.
  • Everything logged and public: attendance, parses, pull counts, boss by boss.
  • Officers who answer "why did we wipe?" with a log link instead of an opinion.
  • Loot handled without drama, and no mandatory alt runs.

How to apply

It takes about ten minutes, and someone will come back to you within a few days.

Say hello

{% if links.discord %}Drop into our Discord and post in the recruitment channel.{% else %}Message an officer in game, or find us on Raider.IO.{% endif %} Tell us what you play and which nights work.

Send your logs

A Warcraft Logs link and your Raider.IO profile. We would rather see an honest 60th percentile than a cherry-picked screenshot.

Have a chat

A short conversation with an officer about the roster, the tier, and where you would fit. Ask us anything — you should be checking us out too.

Come and raid

A few trial nights on the team that suits, with real feedback after each one. If it works for both sides, you are in.
